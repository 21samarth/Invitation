# Behavior inventory

- **Cover:** click/tap the envelope to reveal the invitation; this starts the
  background music where browser autoplay rules permit.
- **Countdown:** time-driven, updates once per second.
- **Scratch panel:** pointer-driven canvas scratch area; reveals after 30% has
  been cleared and triggers confetti.
- **Music button:** click-driven play/pause toggle, fixed at lower right.
- **Directions:** opens the venue Google Maps URL in a new tab.
- **Decoration:** time-driven, randomly placed falling floral/butterfly items.
- **Responsive layout:** cards switch from one column on mobile to 2/3/4
  columns at the original Tailwind responsive breakpoints.
